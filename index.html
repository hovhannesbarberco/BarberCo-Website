The Barber

Old-school craft, modern precision

Hovhannes Barber & Co is the personal brand of Hovhannes, a North Hollywood barber who has been behind the chair since 2018. Old-school standards, modern technique, and the time it takes to get every detail right.

He picked up the clippers in 2018 and hasn't put them down since. Over the years he has sharpened the details that make a cut: skin-tight fades, crisp tapers, clean lines, and razor work finished the old way, with a hot towel and a steady hand. Every appointment is one-on-one, with an honest read on what suits you and a cut that still looks right two weeks later.
